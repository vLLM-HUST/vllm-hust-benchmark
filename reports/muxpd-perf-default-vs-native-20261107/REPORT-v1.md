# MuxPD default mode vs native, TP2 and TP4: single-run performance comparison (2026-11-07)

**This is a single-run comparison: each of the four configurations replayed the 13 load plans once.
There is no pass-to-pass variance, so differences are reported as measured, without significance
claims.** Tracking issue: `vLLM-HUST/vllm-ascend-MuxPD-hust#36`. No answer-quality gate; ROW
(opt-in, default off) was not performance-tested; no TP8 or other models.

## Setup

- Model and stack: Qwen3.5-35B-A3B BF16 on 910B2, Core `d0f22d2`, Ascend `c182a9e`, MuxPD main
  `43fcaf4` (clean). `HCCL_DETERMINISTIC` unset, NPU keepalive stopped during all four arms.
- Arms, run one at a time on the same cards per TP (port 8300, nothing else on the cards):
  - TP2, cards 0,1: MuxPD default, then native.
  - TP4, cards 0-3: MuxPD default, then native.
- Shared arguments: max-num-seqs 16, max-model-len 16384, no prefix cache, gpu-memory-utilization
  0.85, compile mode 3, FULL_DECODE_ONLY graphs [1,2,4,8,16].
  - MuxPD default: `scripts/serve-qwen35-muxwise.sh` (async decoupled scheduler/worker, isolated
    overlap q4, decode graph, no chunked prefill, 16384-token budget).
  - Native: `tools/serve-native.sh` with max-num-batched-tokens 16384, chunked prefill on (native
    default), async scheduling at its default.
  - Launch identities: `data/*.identity.txt`.
- Load: the 13 frozen formal plans (`data/formal-plans.SHA256SUMS`, verified before running),
  replayed by `data/loadgen.py`: burst, contend, mixed (rate 1.0 and 1.6) and short (rate 2 and 4,
  overload).
- Goodput: the existing SLO in `data/aggregate.py`, unchanged: a request is good if TTFT \<= 1 s (3
  s for long requests), TPOT \<= 0.1 s and its longest inter-token gap \<= 0.5 s. Goodput = good
  requests / all requests of the plan.
- Raw results: `data/raw-loadgen-results.tar.gz`; summaries: `data/summary.json`, `data/tables.md`,
  `data/single-tp*.txt` (generated by `data/perf36_summary.py`, `data/perf36_single.py`).

## Plan history (all records kept)

The original plan (`data/PLAN.md`) reused the 2026-11-04 native runs and reran MuxPD for 3 passes.
It was revised during the first TP2 MuxPD pass (`data/PLAN-REVISION-1.md`): all four arms measured
fresh, one pass each. The three TP2 MuxPD plans already finished on the running server (burst-s1,
burst-s2, contend-s1) were kept and the remaining ten filled on the same server process; passes 2-3
never ran. An earlier TP2 MuxPD launch was aborted before any plan ran because the keepalive was
restarted during server load (`data/NOTES.md`). Every arm then started on first launch; 0 failed
plans and 0 request errors in all four arms. The one Traceback in the native TP2 server log is the
engine shutdown on the planned SIGTERM after its last plan.

## Gains

- Goodput (existing SLO) is higher on every plan at TP2 (13/13) and on 10/13 at TP4. Pooled over all
  2077 requests: TP2 10.2% -> 34.0%, TP4 12.7% -> 35.2%. The largest changes are on the mixed and
  mixed-r1.6 plans (for example mixed-r1.6-s2: TP2 0.5% -> 64.4%, TP4 1.4% -> 62.5%).
- Decode stalls: worst stall lower on all 13 plans at both TP; ITL p99 on the mixed and short plans
  drops from about 0.53-0.59 s to about 0.09-0.11 s, because a long prefill no longer stalls running
  decodes. This is what lifts Goodput (the 0.5 s gap SLO).
- Throughput: under overload (short-r2/r4) +4.3% to +8.4% at TP2 and +8.8% to +10.4% at TP4;
  mixed-r1.6-s2 +5.3% / +6.2%. TP4 contend +2.5% / +3.3%.
- TPOT: p50 lower on the mixed-r1.6 and short plans at both TP (up to -40%), and p99 lower on all 13
  TP4 plans.
- TTFT under overload: p99 lower on the short plans (TP2 -5% to -8%, TP4 -5% to -16%) and on
  mixed-r1.6; mixed-r1.6-s2 p50 6.0 s -> 1.0 s at both TP.

## Costs

- Light and moderate load TTFT is higher: TTFT p50 +30% to +60% and p99 +17% to +65% on the contend
  and mixed-r1.0 plans at both TP (for example TP2 contend-s1 p50 0.53 -> 0.79 s, p99 0.73 -> 1.14
  s).
- TP2 contend: throughput -6.7% / -7.8%, TPOT p50 +16% / +17%, and TPOT p99 +17% / +27%.
- TP2 burst: TPOT p50 +8% / +19% and TP2 burst-s2 TTFT p99 +18%; TP2 mixed-r1.0-s1 TPOT p50 +8.5%.
- Typical inter-token latency is higher: ITL p50 worse on 13/13 plans at TP2 and 9/13 at TP4 (up to
  about 2x on mixed-r1.6), and ITL p99 is higher on the burst and contend plans (about 0.03-0.05 s
  -> 0.06-0.09 s), where native has no long-prefill stalls to remove.
- TP4 Goodput lower on contend-s1 (100% -> 85.7%), contend-s2 (100% -> 92.9%) and mixed-r1.0-s1
  (98.3% -> 87.4%), from the higher TTFT crossing the 1 s SLO.
- Throughput is within about +-1% on burst and mixed-r1.0 at both TP.

## Validity and limits

- Single run per arm, run sequentially within each TP (MuxPD first). Ordering and cross-time drift
  are not separated from the configuration effect.
- Drift is visible against the 2026-11-04 three-pass runs (reference only, not used above): the new
  native arms show median TPOT p50 +12% (TP2) and +31% (TP4) versus their earlier native runs, and
  the new MuxPD arms +11% and +38% versus earlier MuxPD builds; throughput is about 1-3% lower in
  both. Both arms moved in the same direction, so the within-day comparison above is the usable one,
  but differences of a few percent (burst/mixed-r1.0 throughput, small TPOT changes) are within
  plausible run-to-run and day-to-day variation and should not be read as effects.
- The large, consistent effects (Goodput, worst stall, ITL p99 on mixed/short, overload throughput,
  light-load TTFT increase) have the same sign at TP2 and TP4 and in the earlier three-pass
  comparisons.
- Known configuration differences between arms are by design: MuxPD never chunks prefill, native
  uses chunked prefill; scheduler/worker classes differ.
- Scope: one model, these 13 synthetic plans, TP2 and TP4; no quality measurement in this round.
