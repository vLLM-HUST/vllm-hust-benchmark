# Source lineage and data boundary

This data-only component derives from intellistream/llm-serving-workloads and the
workload-preparation component in intellistream/agent-workload-suite; see MIGRATION_PROVENANCE.json.
Its new distribution is vllm-hust-workload-data and its namespace is vllm_hust_workload_data. It
does not replace the research API.

The maintainer requested this migration and public pull request. This notice records lineage, not a
license grant for third-party datasets, code, weights or benchmark environments. Those assets retain
their own terms and are obtained separately.

No enterprise payloads, private answers, credentials, model weights, research databases, observation
hooks, analysis modules or QBI runtime/history overlays are distributed. Metadata and software
fixtures are not workload execution evidence. Official task registration does not imply an installed
native runner, successful model execution or completed scoring.
